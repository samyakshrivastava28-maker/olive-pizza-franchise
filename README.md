Portal for individual franchise partners to manage branch-level performance, daily P&L statements, branch inventory, staff shifts, settlement records, and localized notifications.

---

## 🌟 Capabilities

- **Branch P&L & Financial Reports**: Daily automated settlement ledger with GST breakdown and export to PDF/Excel.
- **Inventory & Stock Management**: Low-stock thresholds, item availability toggles, and replenishment requests.
- **Staff Shifts**: Cashier and kitchen staff attendance tracking and permission auditing.
- **Access Roles**: Authorized franchise managers and owner accounts (`webhub2811@gmail.com`, `olivepizzarjn@gmail.com`).
